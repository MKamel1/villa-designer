Read [the roadmap](../../../docs/ROADMAP.md) and the relevant stage in
[the method](../../../docs/method/villa-design-method.md).

Identify the decision being made, its stage, the supplied evidence and
the decisions it would invalidate. Use existing brief/site/feasibility
commands and cited rule tools for measurements. Recommend a next action
with its reason and consequences. Do not replace deterministic arithmetic
with agent judgement.

The bedroom demonstrates tools; its dimensions, scope exclusions, proxies
and lighting targets do not establish a villa brief or a statutory code.
Keep missing real inputs visible. Preserve existing authorization for
implementation while keeping client design approvals explicit.

Use [the learning index](../../../docs/LEARNINGS.md) when an observed
failure changes the process. A reproduced bug needs a regression; an
architectural choice needs a decision record. Keep the broader roadmap
current after completing an example capability.

Use [expert guidance](../../../docs/guidance/README.md) and the numbered stage package for this decision. `stage_context` returns inputs and evidence; `lookup_evidence` retrieves scoped passages and precedents; `review_stage` reports unresolved evidence and approval readiness. Existing numerical targets remain diagnostic until verified against original editions. Use the source curator for acquisition and verification and the architectural critic for an independent challenge. Keep client taste project-local.
