# 3D Model Candidate Research (Round 8)

## Executive Summary
This research phase surveys free, permissive-licence (CC0 and CC-BY) 3D assets to replace procedural geometric stand-ins in Villa Zayed D1. Three furniture targets were investigated:
1. **Kids Room A bunk bed** (Target 1): 900 × 2000 mm mattresses, ~1.70 m overall height, lower mattress ~0.40 m, upper ~1.40 m, with long-side ladder and upper safety rail.
2. **Kids Room B bed** (Target 2): 1200 × 2000 mm three-quarter mattress, ~0.95 m headboard height, simple modern Scandinavian style.
3. **Parents' vanity chair / stool** (Target 3): compact seat for a 1.00 × 0.40 m vanity with 0.75 m top height, ~0.45 m seat height, sliding under the 0.40 m top.

Every model previously catalogued across project reports was strictly excluded. All candidates are documented below with verifiable metrics, exact license declarations, and fit risk evaluations.

---

## Scope and Methodology

- **Licencing Strictness**: Only public domain ([CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)) or Creative Commons Attribution ([CC-BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)) downloadable models were accepted. Non-Commercial (CC-BY-NC, CC-BY-NC-SA), No-Derivatives (CC-BY-ND), and proprietary "Free Standard" models were screened out and rejected.
- **Dimensional Demarcation**: Following user guidelines and project truth discipline, stated dimensions are only recorded when verbatim quoted from the author's metadata or official manufacturer specifications. Where dimensions are absent, they are explicitly designated as `"not stated"`—never estimated or guessed from renders.
- **Clean Exclusion**: Cross-referenced against [beds-report.md](file:///C:/Users/mmbka/arch-pipeline-agy2/docs/beds-report.md), [kids-beds-candidates.json](file:///C:/Users/mmbka/arch-pipeline-agy2/docs/kids-beds-candidates.json), [search2-report.md](file:///C:/Users/mmbka/arch-pipeline-agy2/docs/search2-report.md), [desks-report.md](file:///C:/Users/mmbka/arch-pipeline-agy2/docs/desks-report.md), and [storage-report.md](file:///C:/Users/mmbka/arch-pipeline-agy2/docs/storage-report.md).
