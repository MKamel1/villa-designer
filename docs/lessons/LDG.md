# LDG lessons

1. **A per-seat rule set can make a brief infeasible and the generator should say so, not tune around it.** Table 1.2 m clear, pit 1.5 m from seats and 2 m from structure, 3.46 m2 per dining seat: only 0.5-5.5 % of random packings fit the 53 m2 garden. Guard: `candidate-counts.json` records packed vs sampled per cell; `programme.json` states what does not fit.
2. **A check added late can empty a cell.** The canopy-spread check removed every south tree and then every W and H planted variant (a tree the sampler still placed). Found because survivors read 0; guard: survivors per cell are printed and the run is only accepted when every (idea, variant) has a package (`test_outputs_zero_fails_and_traceable` needs all summary entries).
3. **Planting that picks species before hydrozone breaks the hydrozone check** (olive low + bed plants medium). Guard: `plant_bed(force_hz=...)` and `LDG-HYDRO` count trees in the bed.
4. **A guard against the real defect, not a synthetic one:** olive passed every LDK rule and was drawn at 7.6 m spread over the sister plot's corner; `test_tree_spread_check_catches_olive` fails without the spread check.
5. **Rounding in rectangle decomposition made phantom overlaps** (0.001 m slivers failed GEOM for 80 % of the wall candidates). Guard: overlap threshold 5e-3 m2.
6. **A score that cannot discriminate hides nothing but also helps nothing** (the `view` term was 1.0 for every candidate). Replaced by the mean rooms-seeing over the beds. Per-concept court picks did not diversify because planted variants of one structure score alike: next time pick distinct structures.
7. **Furniture-first packing produces furniture diagrams.** Pass 1 sampled furniture and then drew beds in the leftovers: a 0.6 m perimeter strip, no tree, identical courts. Pass 2 fixes bones first (typology) and the arithmetic of the rule distances (pit 2.0 m from everything, 1.5 m from seats, table 1.2 m) is worked out before anything is drawn. Guard: `test_concepts_are_distinct_in_structure` (pit, tree, bed ids and court beds differ between concepts) and `test_each_south_concept_has_a_canopy_tree_in_the_precedent_range`.
8. **The critics take the first zone that contains a point.** My dining zone (27.7 m2, required by LS-DINE-AREA at 3.46 m2 per seat) overlapped every other room, so U-GATHER-GROUPS read "dining seats 23, lounge seats 0" and U-QUIET-SOUTH "no quiet seat". Found by reading the evidence string, not the verdict. Guard: `assemble()` orders zones most-specific-first; `test_critics_classify_seats_into_dining_lounge_quiet` fails with the old order (quiet seats vanish).
9. **LDK grades some shortfalls "warn", and pass 1 counted only "fail".** LS-FIRE-SEAT (500 mm against 1500), LS-ROOTZONE and LS-FIRE-STRUCT were all `warn`; a hard-fail filter on `fail` would have let them through. Guard: `HARD_WARN` in `ty_run.py` and the negative tests `test_fire_pit_too_near_table_fails`, `test_tree_over_paving_fails_rootzone`, `test_mixed_hydrozone_bed_fails` (each failed first with the old filter).
10. **A published rule can penalise a good built-in.** A banquette-table unit on the rear wall measured 0 mm clear (the wall counts as an obstruction), and LS-DINE-AREA scales a 4-seat figure linearly (27.7 m2 for 8 seats against about 15.8 m2 by the Time-Saver construction). Recorded as limits in REPORT.md rather than worked around; the dining zone is the shared central court.
11. **Judge privacy where people sit.** The big dining zone made every privacy check fail (53 % remaining) although the table was covered; the check now uses the table and bench envelopes. Declared in REPORT.md so it is not read as a quiet relaxation.
12. **Plan symbols must follow the planting density.** Species with small minimum spreads (Acorus 0.15 m) drew as dots and the drifts never merged; plants are now drawn at their planting spacing when that exceeds the mature spread. Check: `test_lone_dot_planting_is_avoided` (every border has a species with at least 3 plants).
13. **A merged lane breaks the ownership guard.** Merging origin/lane/LDC into lane/LDG made `lane_guard` list LDC's files as ours. Keep the lane branch free of other lanes' files; render with the other lane's checkout when needed.
14. **A floor from a wood-fire bylaw must not silently bind, or silently vanish, for a gas product.** LS-FIRE-STRUCT (2.0 m, Calgary/IFC) is the LDK published floor; the shortlisted gas manuals state 0.6-1.2 m sideways and 1.83-2.0 m overhead. The pit's shortfall is reported as a warning, kept in `superseded_by_manual`, and replaced by `LDG-FIRE-MANUAL` (strictest manual figure, 1.25 m / 2.0 m); the BBQ keeps the 2 m rule. Guards: `test_fire_pit_is_on_the_west_line_and_manual_replaces_floor_visibly`, `test_planting_inside_fire_clearance_fails`, `test_pergola_over_pit_fails_overhead` (my first negative test used a 2.7 m pergola, which the manual figure allows: the test, not the check, was wrong), `test_design_clearances_cover_every_shortlisted_manual`.
15. **An opening in a screen breaks the unbroken-screen assumption of the privacy check.** The pass-2 check read 21 % remaining at the table; honouring the opening gives 58 % (limit 30 %). The opening is the client's decision, so it is a visible waiver (`LDG-PRIV-OPENING`, with the figure and the closable-leaf remedy), not a suppression. Guard: `eyes_open` and the programme table carry both numbers.
16. **A ground-level sun layer under-reads a roof-level climber.** The grape strip at the rear fence foot gets 2.5 h on the ground layer (SUNFIT fail) but 5.58 h at foliage level (2.4 m). Guard: `leaf_sun()` (ray test at leaf height) feeds `LDG-SUNFIT`; the critic's H-SUN-FIT still reads the ground layer and is reported as a known limit.
17. **Deciduousness only matters where the geometry lets it.** Winter sun at the table comes from the sides under the roof plane (altitude below 40 degrees), so a bare vine gains no winter sun and the leafed vine loses none; what the vine changes is summer midday sun (3.54 h to 0.89 h) and, bare, the overhead privacy (21 % to 60 % remaining). Hence the louvred frame under the vine. Guards: `LDG-GRAPE-SEASON`, `test_grape_summer_shade_winter_unchanged`, `test_vine_with_no_summer_leaf_fails_season_check`.
18. **The zone-priority defect of pass 2 (lesson 8) came back** (dining zone over the rear bench: U-GATHER-GROUPS "lounge seats 0"). Lesson 8 had only prose. Guard added now: `LDG-ZONE-CLASS` + `test_dining_zone_swallowing_the_rear_bench_fails`.
19. **LDB findings are defects of the layout, so each got an automatic layout guard** (tree in pergola, plant in solid, pot at fence, pond section, duplicate ids, undeclared surface, gravel zone over paving), proven on the real defect (the LDB concept A numbers) in `tests/landscape/concept/test_final.py`, and the LDB build itself is run in the tests (`test_build_lane_finds_no_clip_gap_or_warning`).
20. **A tool run queued behind other lanes' GPU jobs looks like a hang.** The massing job sat `queued` for tens of minutes behind other lanes; check `~/archpipe/queue/jobs/<id>/status.json` before assuming a fault, and render plans (CPU) first.

## Round 3 (shade trees instead of pergolas)
21. **Removing a roof does not remove the roof's duties: privacy, shade and the BBQ moved with it.** The pergolas gave 100 % overhead cover; one tree gave 40-60 % and the first layout failed table, rear bench, quiet bench and east bench privacy. Guard: `LDG-PRIV` summer is hard per seat with the crown as 0.25 m cells (`crown_cells`), `test_without_the_trees_summer_privacy_fails_at_the_table`, and the privacy table (no tree / summer / winter) is in the report.
22. **A tree's rules are three-dimensional but the LDK rules are plan-only.** Crowns count as combustible planting (LS-FIRE-STRUCT 2.0 m) and fill the paving dripline (LS-ROOTZONE): with the trees in, no BBQ position existed and every paved square in a dripline failed. Resolved by stating the BBQ's own manual (`LDG-BBQ-MANUAL`, Weber Q1200, 24 in; not beneath overhead construction; product not chosen) and by gravel floors under the crowns. Guard: `test_bbq_under_a_crown_or_against_planting_fails`, `LDG-GRAVEL-ZONE`, `LS-ROOTZONE` in the hard list.
23. **A pruned crown is a maintenance specification, not a species fact, so the layout carries it with its regime and source status.** NHBC passes for the crape only at 4.5 m (3.38 m needed against 3.45 m), not at the species 9.1 m (6.8 m). Guard: `LDG-PRUNE` fails a pruned tree with no regime (`test_pruned_tree_without_a_regime_fails`); the olive's pruning source is a stated GAP.
24. **A leaning crown is a design device only if every check reads the crown, not the trunk.** The privacy, fire, spread and sun checks use `crown_center`; the LDK rules and the LDC critics read the trunk (conservative for fire and rootzone, non-conservative for the critics' cover). Not hidden: the critics are report-only.
25. **Clearance rules couple to the table's extension.** Moving the table to clear the BBQ broke the 10-seat extension (900 mm to the wall bed, then 1060 mm to the bench). Found by `test_dining_8_extends_to_10_not_12`, which exists because the extension is a client decision; fixed by a 0.31 m wall strip where the table reaches and a shorter rear bench.
26. **Cairo leaf-drop months were a search miss, not a fact.** Four searches gave habit statements only; the months stay a GAP and the client's Dec-Feb is an assumption in the report. Guard: `final_trees.PHENO` carries the evidence string per species and the report prints it.
27. **The tree-spread check assumed one garden box.** The east yard olive (x 21.4) was read as a south-garden tree and failed LDG-SPREAD; the check now knows the yard's own walls. Guard: the east tree is in `test_no_pergola_and_shade_trees_instead` and the hard-fail test.
