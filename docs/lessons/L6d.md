# L6d lessons (defect -> why it was missed -> guard)

| defect | why it was missed | guard now |
|---|---|---|
| Production Cycles threw the tilted, asymmetric wallwasher 90 deg away from the engine (+35.6 % total, 153 % pattern error on LV's single-light probe). | The azimuth offset of `villa_scene.add_light` (`to_track_quat` + a fixed 90 deg) was calibrated on straight-down lights only, where roll about the aim is invisible; the track quaternion's roll for a tilted aim differs from the engine's. | `ies_rotation`: the light's matrix is the engine's own `Placement.rotation()` composed with the calibrated Rz(90), for every aim and spin. `tests/lightcalc/test_light_orientation.py` (13 cases incl. tilts at all four azimuths, the straight-down matrices unchanged, and a negative: the old construction differs for the wallwasher's aim). Rendered proof: LV's `v5_engines.kind_orientation` on every kind. |
| Cycles read Lucy 7.7 % low, the wallwasher 7.5 % low and (through generic strips) the parents' bedroom 4 % low. | The 1.2 % calibration of `IES_POINT_POWER` was done on single-plane files. Blender 4.5 renders a Type-C file with six or more horizontal planes 8.6 % dimmer than the same candela table written as one plane (a pure scale on every point). Found by writing one ERCO downlight table as 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 9, 37 planes (-1.2, -1.2, -5.1, -7.3, -8.0, -8.3, -8.6 %). | `IES_MULTIPLANE_GAIN` (1.094 for >= 6 planes), `test_multiplane_files_get_the_measured_gain...`; LV's probe now reads DESK +1.0 %, WW +1.2 %. |
| LV's V5 probe cache hid the fix: with the renderer changed the probe returned the old numbers. | `run_cycles` keys its cache and its jobq id on the job JSON only, not on the renderer source (`villa_scene.py`). Same family as every "re-run did not re-run" trap. | (LV's file, not edited) worked around with a nonce in the job; **LV should hash the renderer sources into the job key**. Before any LV re-run after a renderer change, remove `~/archpipe/queue/jobs/lv-cycles-*` ids of the probe. |
| The ADJ probe showed -9 % to +2 % depending on the roll of the light about its own axis. | A 5.7 deg beam sampled by 25 points 1 m apart: one grid point sits on the edge of the spot, so the L1 is the grid's sampling noise, not an orientation or flux error (the file is axisymmetric). | The new ADJ (28 deg) reads -4.8 % / L1 5.6 %; the probe bar (L1 <= 0.15, sum <= 0.10) is not tightened for narrow beams. |
| LA4 passed a bed downlight at 89 953 cd/m2 (cap 3000) and an entry-lobby downlight at 122 595 cd/m2. | The only reclined sightline looked toward the foot of the bed (17 deg up), so the pillow-end downlight was 53.7 deg from it, outside the 45 deg cone; "pass" was a property of the sightline. A rebind changes angular reach, not only lumens (see L6c). | `VC.bed_lying_sightlines`: the eye on each pillow looking straight up, every bed of every bedroom (parents' two pillows, kids' B, the bunk's both ends), policy card `L6-POLICY-LA4-bed-lying-looking-up`. `tests/lightcalc/test_la4_bed_lying.py`: today's positions fail (118 820 / 122 595 / 113 931 cd/m2), the old sightline passes them (the gap), the design passes, a DL 2.3 m away and a shielded one stay quiet. The cone is 2.0 m in plan at 2.0 m height: a DL must be >= 2.0 m from every pillow eye. |
| The 8 % bar of the radiosity test was set to fit the pantry. | The pantry's Radiance plane is 8 points inset 0.3-1.4 m from the ends of a 2.9 x 1.0 m room; the radiosity averaged the whole floor, whose dark ends the plane never sees. At the plane's own points the same solver gives -1.0 / -0.7 % (16 / 24 patches); the "divergence" with finer patches (-7.0 / -8.7 / -9.3 %) is the ends being resolved. Not a transport error. | Bar back to 6 % for the three converging rooms; `radiosity.floor_lux_at` (footprint-matched) at 6 % for all four rooms; the pantry's area-mean comparison is a strict `xfail` with the numbers. |
| The 1.5 m Jilly (3558 lm) ran at 3.9 % of its output in the Evening scene; the island globes at 14 % in Reading. | No check compared zone output fractions with the product's range; each scene level was set for its look and the driver for its card. | `test_every_zone_runs_at_15_percent_or_more_outside_the_night_and_film_scenes` (scene level x driver, every zone and scene), `test_night_and_film_levels_stay_above_the_dimming_floor_of_the_spec`, `test_table_pendant_is_scaled_to_the_table` (pendant <= 2/3 of the table; rated <= 933 lm). |
| ADJ bought on an unexplained beam discrepancy (published 4.7 deg, file 5.7). | V10 "partial" was reported but did not gate the choice; the pessimistic reading (driver 1.11 for the armchair) was only computed by hand. | `test_every_bound_product_is_v10_pass`; `test_armchair_card_is_met_at_or_below_080_under_the_pessimistic_beam` (beam 20 % narrower, both with Imax kept and with flux kept). |
| V7 grid pitches (corridor 0.85 m, -6.23 %; bedroom 0.43 m, -3.39 %) were the EN 12464-1 cell rule, never tested for convergence by the engine. | The engine's own grid tests covered the cinema and the daybed only after LV found them. | `ROOM_PLANE_SPACING` 0.30 m (corridor) and 0.20 m (bedroom); `test_l6d_grids_converge_under_2_percent` and its negative (0.85 m is visibly unstable). The bedroom's -3.39 % was a hot spot of the old bed DL under a coarse grid: with the DL at the foot of the bed even 0.43 m gives +0.6 %; the finer pitch is kept as the guard. |
| Removing the entry-lobby downlight for LA4 would have left the lobby unmeasured (4 lx direct from the rest). | The lobby had no calculation plane: a fixture with a card but no plane is never checked. | `room-parents-entry` plane (passage card, min 30 lx): 71 lx mean, 57 lx min with the cove; sensor obstructions regenerated. |
