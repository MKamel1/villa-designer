## Shared fit module

[`archpipe.furniture_fit`](../src/archpipe/furniture_fit.py) now owns measured
bounds, declared source-unit conversion, contents retention, plan-factor
limits, and independent vertical arithmetic. Beds call it through their
existing APIs and retain mattress/berth/headboard, placement, cloth and C8
checks. C8 means the geometry-topology room review.

Plan width and depth factors must each be 0.5–2.0 after unit conversion;
larger divided by smaller minus one must not exceed 0.15 (15 percent), in
either axis order. All design distances are metres. This retains the client
rule from [beds-report.md](beds-report.md), rather than introducing an
ergonomic standard. Unit conversion requires an identified declaration;
missing evidence means the glTF metre convention, never a factor inferred
from a target. New furniture intake rejects an explicit empty unit record;
the bed adapter preserves its historical empty-record default.

`fit_furniture` measures retained converted faces. New contents contracts
require `item-only`, a review basis and explicit omissions of separately
named extras; fused extras refuse. Existing `bed-only` contracts also work.
The vertical factor derives from actual named desktop/seat geometry above
the retained item bottom and a `design` or `citation` source. Required
additional datums, such as chair-back height, use `height_constraints` and
must fit that same transform. No leg, seat or back is stretched separately.
The result reports overall height; fitting the footprint alone is not
acceptance. No new ergonomic height was invented for this stage.

The shared solver is intake arithmetic. Later desk/chair activation still
needs imported-transform verification, reviewed neutral and room previews,
all required heights and full room checks. The legacy renderer's chair
candidate selection still uses its existing product-size proxies and
`place_model` path; Stage 1 does not wire this new contract into activation
or certify those existing candidates. See the repeatable
[furniture workflow](ops/furniture-fit.md).

## Candidate search and remaining gap

[desks-candidates.json](desks-candidates.json) groups eight unique models
over nine footprint/use/style types, with at most three candidates per type.
It records UID (Sketchfab unique model identifier), URL, licence, author,
full credit, metadata receipts, inspected-thumbnail item-only screening,
fit rationale, reservations and pending gates. CC0 means public domain;
CC-BY means Creative Commons Attribution. All shortlisted models currently
list downloadable CC-BY permission; preserve the recorded credits with
later renders and note transformations/omissions.

Study leads use walnut-appearance timber desks and mid-century leather task
seating. Kids desk leads use light-oak appearances. Parents vanity follows
the recorded Warm Luxury Minimalism/walnut scheme; cinema follows existing
warm contemporary oak and charcoal finishes. Styles are traced to the task,
`taste.json`, or existing `villa_render.part_material` records in the JSON.
These are appearance leads, not manufacturer-verified buyable products.

Recommended first downloads are Leaves 110×45 WN for the shared study and
parents vanity, Leaves 110×60 WN for the adult desk, Leaves 120×60 OAK for
kids/cinema, and Flannigan for study/cinema task seating. Numbers in titles
are search hints, not measured dimensions. Source extents, desktop/seat
parts and heights are unverified; any fit can still fail. The compact chair
footprint is a particular intake risk: caster bases and arms must fit without
more than 15 percent plan distortion or independently stretched parts.

**No exact light-oak task-chair candidate was found for the kids rooms.**
Scandinavian/wood/oak office-chair searches mostly returned dining chairs,
lounge chairs, dark upholstery or sets. The last Wooden Office Chair lead
is visibly antique, distressed wood with a red seat, outside the recorded
light Scandinavian palette. A white leather/chrome caster chair is recorded
as an excluded style compromise, not silently treated as light oak. The
kids task-chair group stays empty with its explicit search gap. Existing
chairs remain in place; no substitute style is approved.
