## What was designed, and why this order

Safety first (client brief, `LS-POND-EDGE-LIGHT`): both koi ponds' margins, the fire-pit surround and the main path carry a verified, dimmable product and are met in **every** scene, even "quiet", at 4-11 % of the product's output.
Use scenes second (dining under the crape, gathering, quiet north court, winter evening). Beauty last (tree uplights, climber walls grazed from above), and it is allowed to be unverified because no safety plane depends on it.

Function and beauty per space, with the client's aesthetic (flush/concealed for ambient, spots and pendants for task and centrepieces):

| space | function (sourced) | beauty |
|---|---|---|
| north court + pond | edge >= 4 lx, 8 lx recommended for over-65s (IES HB10 T33.2, pool-deck analogue, as the LS rule cites) | calm: the pond margin at 8 % output and nothing else; no uplight, no wall wash; the koi read through the water |
| east yard + pond | the same, plus the service path | the margin lit from the house face and the south wall, nothing on the bench |
| south garden dining | policy 30 lx avg / 15 min (no published outdoor figure) | two downlights over the table in the crape's lower branches (moonlighting through the leaves), warm uplight on the crape and olive crowns |
| fire pit (shared line) | edge ring >= 4 / 8 lx | lit from two screen posts, in winter the main light of the garden |
| paths | 5 lx, Uo 0.25 (SLL T3.5, workplace analogue) | 3 facade downlights, low |
| climber walls | none | three downlights 0.15 m off the wall graze the star-jasmine from 3.4 m |

**No ambient luminaire is flush or concealed in the ground** because no 2700 K / CRI >= 90 / DALI / IP67 in-ground product with a downloadable photometry file was found (below). Everything is wall-, post- or branch-mounted, shielded, aimed down.

## Obtrusive light and privacy (SLL Table 3.2, zone E3, post-curfew column: Ev 2 lx, I 1000 cd, ULR 0.15; pre-curfew 10 lx / 10 000 cd)

ILP GN01 and CIE 150 are **not held**: no number in this lane is quoted from them (card `LG-OBTRUSIVE-GN01-CIE150-not-held`, status unverified). The E3 limits are SLL's (held). "Residential suburb" is E3; the zone is an assumption (no survey of the neighbours).

Sensors: east and rear neighbours and the east-yard neighbour at ground-floor (ground + 1.5 m) and first-floor (+ 4.5 m) window heights; the sister villa's own facade (the pit is shared by design, so its garden side is not a boundary); the street side; and the **apartment above** = the house's first-floor facade row. Heights are ASSUMED (no survey).

| scene | max Ev on any sensor (lx) | worst group | I toward a sensor (cd) | upward flux to the sky (after crowns) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| summer-night dining | 0.39 | apartment-above 0.39 | 181 | 0.122 (UP-3) |
| social gathering | 0.59 | apartment-above 0.59 | 254 | 0.122 |
| quiet north court | 0.02 | sister facade | 6 | 0.001 |
| winter evening | 0.05 | sister facade | 72 | 0.001 |

All under the post-curfew limits (2 lx, 1000 cd, 0.15), with no curfew switch-off needed. The ground-floor neighbour windows get **exactly zero**: every boundary is a 4 m wall (the test removes the wall and light arrives). The apartment above sees at most 0.59 lx, from the uplights, **without** the crown stopping any of it (the engine counts no foliage in Ev: conservative).
All Iku are downlights: ULR 0.005 or less in the installed attitude. The three uplights are ULR 1.0 by definition; the check counts only the flux of rays that miss the opaque (leaf-on) crown ellipsoid: 1.7 %, 1.5 % and 12.2 % (the east-yard olive, the smallest crown, 2.6 m).
**In winter the crape is bare, so the winter scene switches the uplights off** (and the olive crowns are evergreen but the scene keeps them off for calm). A bare crown would otherwise let the beam out: this is a seasonal operating rule that must reach the commissioning notes.
Glare (policy 3000 cd/m2 within 45 deg of a level sightline from 8 seated or standing eye points to the table, pit, door and pond): worst 2163 cd/m2 (summer), 2884 (gathering, DN-1, 4 % margin), 387 (quiet, winter).

## Deviations from the client rules (stated, not hidden)

* **CCT**: the verified product's file is 3000 K (2700 K on request, no file). **Night 2200 K cannot be met**: a fixed-CCT LED does not shift with dimming; dim-to-warm needs a DT8 product no found exterior product offers.
* **CRI >= 90**: Iku 92. Uplight unspecified (requirement).
* **No phase-cut**: Iku is DALI-2 (datasheet); drivers are in the fitting.
* **Driver <= 0.80**: met everywhere.
* **Flush/concealed**: nothing is flush in the ground (no verified product), so the "flush fittings for ambient" aesthetic is met on walls and screens only.
