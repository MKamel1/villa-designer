# Dining pendant (client decision, 2026-11-22)

The single iGuzzini QA03 (ø59 × 250 mm) meets the lighting cards but reads as an afterthought over the 1.8 m table, next to the three island globes. The client chose a **larger decorative pendant scaled to the table** (about 1.2 m long, policy: ≤ 2/3 of table length), with the task (downlight) function kept separate where needed. Client rules unchanged: 2700 K, CRI ≥ 90, DALI-2, no phase-cut, ≤ 0.80 driver, every scene ≥ 15 %, V10-verifiable.
