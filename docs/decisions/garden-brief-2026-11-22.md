# Garden brief (client decision, 2026-11-22)

Recorded by the lead from the client's answers; the landscape method (docs/quality/LANDSCAPE-METHOD.md) is APPROVED by the client (2026-11-22).

## Uses
- **South garden**: winter daytime stays and activities; summer nights; gatherings and social events for **~10–20 people** (a dining group plus a lounge group, with standing room). It must ALSO provide relaxation (a quieter corner within it).
- **North garden**: relaxation and quiet time.

## Features
- **Fire pit or heater** in the south garden, for winter evenings.
- **Small BBQ** in the south garden; it may be portable (a stand place with a hard surface, clear of planting and the facade; no built outdoor kitchen required).
- **Small koi pond** with lush green planting in a tropical or Japanese style, in the north garden OR in front of the dirty kitchen. The designers propose the location, and the client chooses at the concept gate.
- **Herbs and fruit**: a herb bed and/or fruit trees.

## Constraints
- **Privacy is critical**: strong screening from the neighbours and from the apartment above.
- **Maintenance: HIGH** is acceptable (weekly gardener). Lawn, seasonal colour, pots and edibles are all allowed; artificial grass is no longer the default and becomes a choice per area.
- Standing decisions still apply: shade over sun; any beautiful, shading, site-viable plant (not only Mediterranean); colourful drifts; frangipani; terracotta pots; no pricing yet.

## Professional and site items (outside the method)
- Koi pond: filtration, pump, depth and liner, and fish welfare (a pond supplier sizes the filtration; we give the volume and depth).
- Retaining walls over about 0.6 m.
- Soil and drainage tests.
- Irrigation hydraulics.
- Nursery availability in Egypt.
- Roots near foundations of unknown depth.
- Fire pit: clearance to the facade and to planting follows the product's instructions.
