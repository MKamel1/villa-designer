# Privacy requirement (client, 2026-11-26)

The residents are a conservative Muslim family. Privacy is a design requirement across the villa, not only in the parents' suite. The client asked to finalise the parents' suite vestibule (annexing the corridor end past kids room B), to check privacy, and to suggest solutions that improve it.

Requirements are stated by the client. They are the source for these points; no external standard is claimed.

- **Visual privacy of private rooms:** no sightline from guest-reachable or public points (front door, entrance, guest sitting areas, guest WC, stair landing, corridor, kids' doorways, outdoor areas, the sister villa and neighbours) into bedrooms, dressing rooms or bathrooms, with doors open or closed.
- **Guest and family separation:** guests can be received without seeing or passing family zones. The women of the family can move between private rooms without being seen from guest areas.
- **Overlooking:** windows and terraces must not overlook the neighbours' or the sister villa's private areas, and must not be overlooked by them.
- **WC orientation:** no qibla restriction (client, 2026-11-26: "no need for the qibla restrictions"). The audit does not check it.

**Priority (client, 2026-11-26):** the main privacy restriction is around the PARENTS' SUITE: the bedroom, dressing room, ensuite and the new vestibule. Solve it fully first, with zero sightlines from anywhere outside the suite, including from family areas such as the kids' rooms and doorways, the corridor and the stair landing. The rest of the villa is OUT OF SCOPE (client: "no need to clear the whole villa, just the parent room privacy restriction").

**The exact scenario (client, 2026-11-26):** someone comes out of the ensuite into the dressing room believing they are in a closed room. If the dressing room's door and the bedroom/suite door are also open, they can be seen from far away (from the corridor). DESIGN CRITERION: design for the worst case, with EVERY door of the suite left open. There must be NO line of sight from any point outside the suite to:
- the ensuite door threshold and the zone just outside it;
- the dressing room;
- the route between the ensuite and the dressing room.

A closed door must not be the only protection. Preferred means:
- a dog-leg or offset so that the openings never line up;
- a vestibule with a self-closing door as a second layer, not the first;
- door handing so that an open leaf screens the view.
