# Bedroom: detailed guidance exercise

Use `review_stage` for stages 4, 5, 6 and 7 with
`knowledge/projects/bedroom.json`. It binds the committed measured Revit fixture,
not a newly executed native run. Six furniture objects remain in the review,
including the chair. The room scope cannot establish a villa circulation strategy.

At Rooms, compare the measured furniture footprints, door swing, bed-making,
dressing, desk use and luggage placement. Existing target dimensions are
diagnostic because their original passages are not yet verified. The regression
exercise moves a bedside table into the bed footprint and expects a clash despite
its accessory relationship. This tests a real known failure class, not an invented
architectural minimum.

At Systems, develop a reading scene, a sleep scene and a low-disturbance route to
the bathroom. Ask who reads while someone else sleeps before choosing fixtures
or controls. Record daylight, glare, blackout, acoustics, temperature, ventilation,
power and equipment access per room. Existing direct-light tools supply measured
direct illuminance; their output cannot certify total-light uniformity, acoustic
privacy or thermal comfort. No numerical comfort standard is newly enabled here.

At Substance, take the headboard/bedside junction as the representative detail:
coordinate the timber finish, lamp fixing, power outlet, cable path, removable
driver access and cleaning gap. Show a section through the curtain recess to check
blackout continuity, glazing access and conflicts with the ceiling. Actual product
build-ups and moisture details need manufacturer data; an invented layered sketch
must not be presented as a construction assembly.

At Proof, use the current measured extract and unchanged renderer-input format.
After Claude produces its images, attach content fingerprints for the model,
rendering input and each reviewed image, then compare plans, sections, lighting
report and day/night images. Run the existing rendering-quality checks and visually
inspect the views. Record unresolved issues beside the images and trace the
household requirements to the same revision.

This exercise deliberately returns unresolved readiness. It demonstrates useful
measurement and honest limits without converting the bedroom into a completed
villa or silently approving a real client brief.
