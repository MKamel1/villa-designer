# 3 — Order

This is a working stage package. Methods and examples are project-authored; published support is limited to the evidence cards linked below.

## Inputs and interview

Required: adjacencies, privacy, structure_strategy, service_routes, climate, landscape_strategy. Each fact records value, status and provenance; unknown is not an assumed pass.

- Should guests see the garden immediately or discover it after arrival?
- How should children reach bedrooms when you are hosting?

## Method

1. Compare compact bar, courtyard and separated pavilion organisations using the same brief and area definitions.
2. Draw arrival, adjacency, private circulation, massing and at least one section together; test indoor-outdoor thresholds.
3. Overlay tentative supports, spans, stairs, wet stacks and main service paths. Flag unsized spans; never imply engineering certification.
4. Recommend one with reasons, diagram, evidence, cost/space/maintenance consequences and a client decision. Obtain a separately authored critique.

## Worked example

Pilot recommends a compact garden bar: private hall bypasses living and stacked service core limits distribution. Courtyard improves sheltered outdoor life but uses more envelope; pavilion exposes bedrooms to the hosting route and needs an unresolved long span. See [pilot diagrams](../pilot-concepts.svg) and [bedroom exercise](../bedroom-worked-example.md).

## Checks and failures

- Measured geometry and source-validated targets where available; otherwise unresolved
- Failure: spectacular glazing hides structural depth and glare. Remedy: draw the section with shading, beam zone and curtains; reopen Fit if necessary.
- Failure: bedroom access crosses the party. Remedy: reserve a private hall, then recalculate area.

## Deliverables and approval

Compare every drawn arrival/private/service route with the graph used by the
deterministic check. A graph pass cannot prove room widths, door positions or
that a drawn route avoids another room. See the independent pilot critique.

Deliver: three_concepts, recommendation, plans_sections_elevations, independent_critique.

- Three concepts differ in spatial organisation
- Private and service routes work
- Structure wet stacking and landscape coordinated
- Recommendation addresses critique

Record reviewer, current revision, outcome and reasons for every qualitative criterion. Present the recommendation, household reason, visual explanation, evidence and cost/space/maintenance consequences. Request the client’s explicit decision only on a concrete reviewed proposal. Unresolved evidence or missing facts keep the gate open.
