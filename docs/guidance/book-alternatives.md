# Missing books: what each one blocks, and what to do instead

Checked 2026-09-25. The client asked: get a book only if it is free, otherwise
skip it or name an alternative, and say what each one blocks.

**Only one book blocks a result today: CIBSE TM59. It is free.** The rest are
covered by books already held, or they block only later or optional work.

| Book | Free? | What it blocks today | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CIBSE TM59** (2026 ed., overheating in dwellings) | **Yes.** Standard and member rate both FREE on the CIBSE Knowledge Portal. Log in, add to basket, download the PDF, and accept the sole-use terms. | The **pass/fail on overheating** in the window study. Without it the study reports cooling energy and hours above temperature, but no sourced verdict on whether a room overheats. | **Get it.** Drop the PDF in `archpipe-sources\inbox\`. |
| **BS EN 17037** (daylight) | No. The EVS (Estonian) edition is about €20–50; BSI is the most expensive. | **Not the daylight check.** Room daylight is already verified against SLL Code for Lighting minimum ADF (held). EN 17037 would add sunlight-exposure hours and view-out criteria. | **Skip for now.** Optional later via EVS. |
| **BRE BR 209** (site sunlight) | No. £75 BRE download. | The **sunlight-on-garden/court threshold** and overshadowing of neighbours. The sun-hours can already be computed with our solar tool; only the pass mark would be unsourced. It is UK planning guidance, not binding at the site. | **Skip.** Report garden sun as measured hours without a pass mark. |
| **Neufert, Architects' Data, 6th ed.** | No. The e-book is DRM-locked. | **Nothing on its own.** The unresolved rules that cite Neufert can be re-sourced from held books: DOOR-01 → AD M Table 2.1; DIM-01 → NDSS widths (Metric Handbook); LIGHT-01 → SLL ADF; furniture clearances → Mitton, NKBA, Time-Saver; SAN-01 → UK AD G (free). | **Skip.** Re-source the rules from held books. |
| **Spon's / RSMeans** (costs) | No. Spon's e-book is DRM and expires. | **Relative cost of options**, a later-phase item (W6). Nothing now. | **Skip.** Substitute: AECOM *Middle East Property & Construction Handbook 2026* (free from AECOM), cost per m² for relative comparison. |
| IES RP-11 (US residential lighting) | No. | Nothing: SLL Code for Lighting (held) gives the residential targets. | **Skip.** |
