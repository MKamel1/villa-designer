# Books still missing: how to get them, and what substitutes for them

Checked 2026-09-25. Prices are the sellers' list prices on that date. Drop
any file into `C:\Users\mmbka\archpipe-sources\inbox\`, then run
`scripts/sources.py intake`.

| Missing | Best route | Cheaper or partial substitute | What it unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CIBSE TM59** (overheating in dwellings) | **Free.** The 2026 edition (July 2026) is listed at a standard rate of FREE on the CIBSE Knowledge Portal; register, then check out. Earlier pages mention voucher code `FREE`. | UK Approved Document O (held) names TM59 as its dynamic method. `pythermalcomfort` / Ladybug comfort (open source) implement the adaptive model. | Overheating pass/fail in the window study |
| **BS EN 17037** (daylight in buildings) | Buy a national adoption. The text is identical to BS EN. **EVS-EN 17037:2019+A1:2021** from the Estonian standards body (evs.ee); EVS prices are typically €20–50. SIS (Sweden) charges SEK 1,737; BSI is the most expensive. | BR 209 (2022) applies EN 17037's daylight method for UK practice. The VELUX *Guide to daylighting and EN 17037* is a free secondary summary, not citable for numbers. | Daylight and sunlight targets |
| **BRE BR 209, 2022** (site layout for daylight and sunlight) | BRE bookshop download, £75 (bregroup.com/store). The product page states no DRM terms; confirm at checkout. | Local-authority planning reports quote its numbers widely, but those are secondary; use them only to cross-check. | Sunlight to gardens and rooms, overshadowing |
| **Neufert, Architects' Data, 6th ed.** (Wiley 2023) | Wiley's e-book is DRM-locked. Buy the **paperback** (ISBN 9781119873945) and scan the chapters I list; scans are fine for intake. | **German original, *Bauentwurfslehre*, 44th ed.** (Springer Vieweg 2025, ISBN 978-3-658-44936-0). Springer sells e-books through SpringerLink, usually as watermarked PDF without DRM; confirm on the page. I can read German, so this is the most current Neufert. The Metric Handbook 7th ed. (held) already covers most dwelling dimensions. | Cross-check of room and circulation dimensions |
| **Spon's A&B Price Book 2026** / RSMeans | Spon's print copy includes a VitalSource e-book (DRM, expires Dec 2026). Kobo sells an EPUB, also DRM. Print plus scans of the rates sections works. | **AECOM *Middle East Property & Construction Handbook 2026*** (free from AECOM insights) gives regional cost benchmarks per m². That is enough for the relative cost of options, which is all the method uses cost for. | Relative cost at each gate |
| IES RP-11 (optional) | IES webstore (DRM-locked PDF). | SLL Code for Lighting (held) already gives the residential targets. | US lighting cross-check |

## Recommended order

1. **TM59**: free, so download it now.
2. **EVS-EN 17037**: about €20–50.
3. **BR 209**: £75.
4. ***Bauentwurfslehre* 44th ed.** (Springer), if it is DRM-free; otherwise the Neufert 6th paperback.
5. **AECOM Middle East handbook**: free. Buy Spon's only if item-level rates are needed.
