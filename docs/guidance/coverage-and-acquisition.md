# Phase coverage and acquisition

This framework covers design and visualization for private villas. Hotel references inform comfort and appearance. Local-code research, permits, construction administration and engineering certification are outside scope. “A+ luxury” is the client’s agreed quality brief, not a certification.

Lighting, landscape, materials, structure and service routes begin at concept stage and are refined together.
