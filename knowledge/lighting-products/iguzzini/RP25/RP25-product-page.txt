DESCRIPTION
Direct emission linear modular lighting system with Warm White CRI90 monochrome LED lamps. General Light (High Output) luminaire with Opti-Diamond Space optic available in a White Cover (Transparent white) or Black Cover (Transparent black) version. Complete with 48Vdc Mid-Power Led circuit and PWM control system. Frameless version with extruded aluminium profile; Modular luminaire that can be positioned freely as it rotates 360° around its own axis (See the instruction sheet for the accessories to be used).
