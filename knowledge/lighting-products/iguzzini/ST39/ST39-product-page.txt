Description
Product for linear lighting - with white monochromatic LED with high colour rendering - made on a white flexible circuit. External protection in transparent silicone sheath with end caps. IP65 protection rating for the strip with full length (not sectioned). The ends of the circuits are equipped with connectors with IP20 protection rating, therefore the installer is responsible for adapting the connectors to the insulation. Supplied in 5-metre reels wrapped in special supports; a connection kit for in-line or power connections is included in the package. The strip can be sectioned at 50 mm intervals; in case of intermediate cuts, make sure to carefully seal the cut ends to restore the protection conditions. LED characteristics: white colour 2700K - CRI 90 - 160 LED/m - 120° opening angle - 75 W total - 24 V power supply. Power supplies to be ordered separately. The LED strip features a pre-applied high-adhesion 3M backing tape, ensuring secure and long-lasting attachment to clean, smooth surfaces, and allowing for easy, tool-free installation..
