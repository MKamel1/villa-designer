- `plans/final-south.png` (plan, client north up, house top left): the south garden and the east yard. The west line is at the left with the flush pit (orange disc) between two short screen stubs; each stub has a small planted bed against it (grape and salvia at the top, star jasmine (S9, the key's 'Trachelospermum', 2 plants), justicia and aspidistra at the bottom). Right of the pit are the BBQ square and the table (white rectangle) with its chairs, now under a large pale-green circle (the crape myrtle's 4.0 m head, trunk dot to its right). A second, darker circle at lower right is the olive over the rear and quiet benches, overlapping a third (pomegranate) at the garden's east end. Paving is only the west third; the floor under the crowns is cream gravel. A thin planted strip with climbers runs along the south wall, a mass of palms, jasmine and perennials fills the south-east corner. At the top right the east yard shows the blue pond, a bench, and a fourth round crown (the small olive) over the bench; gravel under it. The yard's south boundary is now a wall (the black bar down the right edge, now continuing the whole length of the yard): star jasmine (S9) at its north-west end, in the far bed with the fatsia and farfugium, and Arabian jasmine (S4) in the divider bed beside the bench and under the olive; the pond-edge acorus strip has none. The plan reads as a planted garden, not a paved terrace; the labels at the right are dense. The plan key calls the crape 'understorey' (its LDL form is shrub): a labelling quirk of the plan style, not a design fact.
- `plans/final-north.png`: the north court. Pond (blue, grille dashed) in the middle, a ring of acorus on the east and north edges, fatsia, palms and aspidistra massed behind it, a bench outline (white rectangle) at the west edge. No tree: the lower third of the court is plain paving and a path patch, the dullest part of the design. The rhapis crown drawn at 1.8 m spread is the largest plant.
- `plans/final-massing-south.png` (clay massing, oblique from the south-east): the two big crowns, crape and olive, merge into one green mass over the table and rear benches and hide them; the pomegranate sphere sits behind them at the east. The massing therefore shows the canopy but not the furniture below it (use the plan for that).
