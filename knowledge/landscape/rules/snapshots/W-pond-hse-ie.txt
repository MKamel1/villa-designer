The list of supported browsers: Chrome Edge FireFox Opera Safari Water safety outside the home It's important to be extra careful when your child is around water. Drowning can happen in silence, in an instant and in a very small amount of water. Anything that collects water can be a danger. This can include a: puddle bucket or container sink water feature, such as a small pond, fountain or bird bath Children who survive nearly drowning often have long-term health effects. For example, brain injuries. Emergency action required: Call 112 or 999 immediately if: your child is in an emergency Ask for an ambulance, the Garda Síochána, the fire service or the Irish Coast Guard. Your child has an extra risk of drowning if they fall into water while wearing a nappy. Their nappy can quickly fill with water. This can take a child under water. Keep your child safe around water outside the home There are things you can do to keep your child safe around water outside your home.
