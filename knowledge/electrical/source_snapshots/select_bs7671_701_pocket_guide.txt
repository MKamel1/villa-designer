This pocket guide provides an overview of Section 701 of 
BS 7671:2018+A2:2022 (IET Wiring Regulations 18th Edition). 
This guide does not cover all of the requirements of Section 701; 
therefore BS 7671 should always be consulted where doubts exist.
Descriptions of the zones and the electrical equipment permitted 
in each are given below and on the back page. Illustrations of the 
zones are provided in the centre pages.
Zone 0 is limited to the interior of the bath tub or shower basin. For 
showers without a basin, the height of zone 0 is 0.10 m and has the 
same horizontal extent as zone 1 (see below).
In zone 0, only electrical equipment meeting all of the following 
requirements is permitted:
	 • The equipment is fixed, permanently connected and has a degree 
of protection of at least IPX7 (against temporary immersion) and
	 •	The equipment complies with the relevant standard and is suitable 
for use in zone 0 according to the manufacturer’s instructions for 
use and mounting and
	 •	The equipment is protected by a SELV source not exceeding 12 
V AC or 30 V DC, the safety source being installed outside zones 
0, 1 and 2. Note: a source as described in Regulation 414.3 (iv) 
shall not be used.
Zone 1 is the area above the bath tub or shower basin to a height of 
2.25 m from the floor or the highest fixed shower head, whichever is 
higher. The space under the bath tub or shower basin is included if 
accessible without the use of a tool.
For showers without a basin, zone 1 extends 1.20 m horizontally 
from the centre point of the fixed water outlet on the wall or ceiling. 
In zone 1, only the following fixed and permanently connected 
electrical equipment having a degree of protection of at least IPX4 
(against splashing from any direction) is permitted, provided it is 
suitable according to the manufacturer’s instructions:
	 •	Electric showers, shower pumps and whirlpool units;
	 •	Ventilation equipment, towel rails, water heaters and luminaires;
	 •	Equipment protected by a SELV or PELV source not exceeding 
25 V AC or 60 V DC (and SELV switches not exceeding 12 V AC 
or 30 V DC), the safety source being installed outside zones 0, 1 
and 2 in each case. Note: a source as described in Regulation 
414.3 (iv) shall not be used.

Zone 2 is the area external to zone 1 for a distance of 0.6 m 
horizontally and a height of 2.25 m from the floor or the highest 
fixed shower head, whichever is higher. For showers without a 
basin, there is no zone 2 but an increased zone 1, as described 
previously.
In zone 2, only the following electrical equipment is permitted:
	 •	Shaver supply units complying with BS EN 61558-2-5, located 
where direct spray from showers is unlikely;
	 •	Other electrical equipment having a degree of protection of at 
least IPX4 (against splashing from any direction);
	 •	Switches and socket-outlets of SELV circuits only, the safety 
source being installed outside zones 0, 1 and 2.
Outside the Zones
Socket-outlets (other than those of SELV circuits noted above) 
are prohibited within a distance of 2.5 m horizontally from the 
boundary of zone 1.
Other equipment, switchgear and accessories in accordance 
with the general requirements of BS 7671 are permitted outside 
the zones (but within the bath/shower room) provided the circuits 
supplying them are protected by one or more 30 mA RCDs (see 
below). 
RCD Protection 
Additional protection by use of one or more 30 mA RCDs must 
be provided for all low voltage circuits:
	 • Serving the location (i.e. the room containing the fixed bath or 
shower);
	 •	Passing through zone 1 and/or zone 2 not serving the location.
Local Supplementary Protective Equipotential Bonding
Where the location containing a bath or shower is in a building 
with a suitable protective equipotential bonding system, 
supplementary  protective equipotential bonding may be omitted 
where all of the following conditions are met:
	 •	All final circuits of the location comply with the requirements 
for automatic disconnection and
	 • All final circuits of the location have additional protection by 
means of one or more 30 mA RCDs and
	 •	All extraneous-conductive-parts of the location are effectively 
connected to the protective equipotential bonding.
Note: Any addition or alteration carried out on an existing 
bath/shower room must meet the requirements of Section 701 of 
BS 7671:2018+A2:2022. In such circumstances unless all three 
conditions noted above are met, local supplementary protective 
equipotential bonding will require to be established.
